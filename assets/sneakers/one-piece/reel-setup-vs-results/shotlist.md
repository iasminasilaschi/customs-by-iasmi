# Reel: "the setup" vs "the results"

The joke: a chaotic home "studio" (One Piece puzzle box as a stand, the screen turned
around as a light, ring light, keyboard, the One Piece cup), then calm, sharp close-ups of
the painted art.

- `01-setup/` and `02-results/` are **copies** of files in `../newer/` (same name after
  the number). The originals stay where they are.
- `vertical/` has 1080×1920 versions of every file, cropped only (no other changes), ready
  for a 9:16 reel.
- Suggested length: about 5.5 s of setup + about 10 s of results = **about 15 s**.

## Notes

- **Audio:** the clips still have the phone's own sound. Mute it under music.
- **Vertical versions:**
  - The portrait clips are already 9:16 (576×1024), so they were only scaled up to
    1080×1920 (about 1.9×, a little soft, fine at reel speed).
  - `02-…low-angle…` comes from a 640×368 landscape clip: a 207×368 slice around the
    shoes, scaled about 5×. Visibly soft, so keep it to a flash.
  - The photos are 3:4, cropped to 9:16 around the art (1152×2048) and scaled down to
    1080×1920, so they're sharp. The landscape setup photo (`04`) is a 864×1536 crop,
    scaled up 1.25×.
