# One Piece sneakers

A gift for a One Piece fan. About a month of work, on and off; worn daily for a
year since then. Website page: `/projects/one-piece-sneakers` (text in
`data/portfolio.ts`, site photos in `public/work/one-piece-sneakers/`).

This folder is the **archive of originals**. The `*-no-background.jpeg` files
lost their transparency in transfer (JPEG can't keep it), so the site uses
cutouts re-made from them with an AI background remover (rembg, BiRefNet model)
and saved as transparent `.webp`.

## Design

- **Right shoe:** One Piece logo on the heel; Zoro on the outer panel, with a wanted poster.
- **Left shoe:** the initials R·M·A on the heel, designed in One Piece logo style
  (my own idea; the details are original, in One Piece spirit); Luffy with a
  dragon on the outer panel.
- Only the outer sides and the heels are painted. He chose the scenes.

## Process & materials

1. Deglazed with acetone + cotton balls
2. Buffed with sandpaper in several grits, for adhesion
3. Brushed clean
4. Sketches
5. Angelus paints in very thin layers, dried in between (heels first)
6. Sealed with Angelus flat finisher + a sofa leather finisher

## Wear test

Worn for a year: rain, sun, day, night, driving, lots of walking ("if I ruin
them, I know who'll fix them"). Brushed clean often. Paint still looks great.
