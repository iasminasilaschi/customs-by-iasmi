# Cherry blossoms, take two

White Nike Air Force 1s with pink Swooshes and cherry blossoms, a birthday
gift for the same best friend as the sushi cats (not named on the site).
Painted twice:

- **before/** First try, April 2023: rushed for a birthday trip to Amsterdam
  (she wanted to wear them in the tulip fields). Swooshes on the evening of
  15 April, pencil sketches 16 April 22:48, first pink layer 17 April 13:00,
  finished 18 April 2:17, plane boarded 19 April 5:40. Not-so-good paints,
  superficial prep, thick layers so the light colours would cover at once.
  The colours bled and mushed, and got onto the fabric collar.
- **after/** Remake, 2024: got them back 27 April, fully cleaned by 7 May
  (acetone, brushes, lots of scrubbing; some spots repainted white; pink
  stays in the seams), finished 10 May with Angelus paints.

Website page: `/projects/cherry-blossoms` (text in `data/portfolio.ts`, site
photos in `public/work/cherry-blossoms/`). Featured on the homepage and in the
hero rotation (`heroShowcase` in `data/media.ts`).
