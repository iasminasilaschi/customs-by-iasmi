# Sushi cats sneakers

Birthday gift for one of my best friends: black Nike Air Force 1s, four sushi
cats (maki, roll, nigiri, nori nigiri), one on each side, plus a gunkan cat on
the box. 22–27 April 2022 (finished at 4:37 a.m.). Website page:
`/projects/sushi-cats` (text in `data/portfolio.ts`, site photos in
`public/work/sushi-cats/`).

## Notes

- Fully my concept; she only chose the shoe (comfy all day, black stays clean).
- First idea (a cat with a fork and knife) was tried on tape and dropped: too
  portrait for the side of a shoe.
- Inspired by sushi-cat artwork found online; I don't remember the artist.
- Process: deglazed only where the cats go, tape outlines, base colours, thin
  layers, line details, sealed. Paints were a leather-paint brand I can't
  remember (not Angelus).
