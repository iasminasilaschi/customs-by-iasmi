## Materials (both caps)

- **Angelus leather paints**: the same ones I use for sneakers
- **Angelus 2-Soft**: ordered for textiles; useful for blending, not for details (see lessons)
- **White graphite transfer paper**: for getting the sketch onto a black cap
- **Angelus flat finisher** (Flat 4-coat): protection, because graduation was outdoors in a park and it might have rained
- **Black paint**: my "eraser" for corrections and sharp edges

## Lessons learned (both caps)

1. **Transfer paper colour matters.** Regular (grey/black) graphite paper is invisible on a black cap. Going over it with white crayon barely helped. **White graphite paper** fixed everything.
2. **2-Soft isn't for caps.** Mixed 1:1 as recommended, it made the white base so thin the black cap soaked it up. Some lettering took about **7 layers**. Its real purpose is flexibility, and caps don't bend. It's lovely for blending but bad for sharp details, which ended up in bare paint.
3. **Black paint corrects.** It's for thin paint that bled past the sketch lines, stray spots, blurry edges, and letters that didn't match.
4. **Finisher if it's outdoors.** Both got Angelus flat finisher.

## Patri — *Doctor cu suflet și rațiune*

("A doctor with soul and reason.") It's pink, delicate and flowery, with a gold-and-pink stethoscope as the
centrepiece and the year as a custom touch at the bottom.

**Brief:** Patri found an inspiration picture online and personalised it with ChatGPT,
with her own words already in place. That picture was the whole brief. I turned AI into reality, 1:1, and I think it came out even better than the reference: accurate,
but real and cute.

**Proudest of:** the **stethoscope shadows**, which make it pop almost 3D. The **tiny white dots** gave it life.

**Fussed over:** the **leaves**, using Angelus *Avocado + Olive* mixed until the colour was right, then different
coloured edges and details. The **flowers** took many pinks and darker burgundy shades to match the reference.

**Correction story:** the two **o's in "doctor"** looked like cousins, not sisters, so I reshaped them with black.

## Andreea — *You have a superpower, and it's called empathy*

It's an anatomical heart with flowers growing out of it like a vase, with hand-lettered text beside it.

**Brief:** Andreea found the heart-vase design on Pinterest and the text on a different
Pinterest cap. She wanted the design from one and the words from the other, and fitting them together was my job.

**Correction story:** the first draft had the text **too low and off-centre** ("can't we just delete a word?").
I erased the whole text and redrew it to sit properly next to the heart.

**Most fun:** **blending**. The heart's shadows and very subtle highlights needed to feel cohesive. On the flowers I blended pink
edges into white petal centres, with a little 2-Soft to make the paint flow.
